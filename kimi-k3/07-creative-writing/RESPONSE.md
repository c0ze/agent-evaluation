The arc moves from dutiful detachment to chosen devotion: June begins as a careful custodian of other people's regrets, treating the future message as evidence — proof that survival and staying will be permitted. Hearing her own unsent plea aloud cracks that passivity, and the message's meaning turns: not a guarantee that she is spared, but an inheritance, a job description handed back to her by the self she is willing to become. Grief is never discharged; it is re-tasked, ending in a small, salt-bright act of hope.
