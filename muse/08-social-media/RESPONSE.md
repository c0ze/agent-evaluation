# Voice choices

Precise over grandiose: every post names one verifiable mechanism (click a sentence, read the passage) instead of claiming speed or intelligence. Warm and mildly nerdy: "where the map breaks down" and "usual suspect" keep it human without hype. Honest about limits: Linux is "investigating, not promised," and there is no lab plan yet, so interest is logged rather than converted into a commitment. Replies stay platform-shaped — fuller on LinkedIn, terse on Bluesky — and the outage reply pairs apology with a concrete next step while keeping account details off the thread.
