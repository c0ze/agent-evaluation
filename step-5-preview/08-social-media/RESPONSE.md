# RESPONSE.md

Voice follows BRAND.md: precise, warm, mildly nerdy, never grandiose.

Each platform gets its own register. LinkedIn carries the launch explanation and answers the risk a buyer actually has — that this is a chatbot wearing a hat — by naming the mechanism instead of the benefit. Bluesky is compressed, and the unglamorous detail (Linux being looked at, not promised) stays in on purpose. Instagram opens on a scene someone recognises rather than a feature list.

The replies share one rule: state what is true today, then stop. No customer counts, no certifications, no dates. Where we don't know — Japanese extraction, institutional seats, a stuck index — we say so and give the smallest honest next step: what to send us, or how to test it themselves. The outage reply moves things out of public comments, because order and account details shouldn't be posted there.
