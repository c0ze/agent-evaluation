# Service update

Customers using our European service experienced slower checkout and delays in background processing. We detected the delays at 09:11 UTC.

We turned off a recently introduced setting at 09:43 UTC, and processing began to recover shortly afterward. The backlog cleared by 10:18 UTC.

Our investigation so far points to the setting affecting how jobs wait before trying again. We’re working to confirm why that happened and to prevent similar delays.
