# Update: checkout and job delays in Europe — resolved

Earlier today, some jobs and checkouts in our European region were delayed
while a processing backlog built up. The backlog has now cleared, and
service is operating normally.

Early evidence suggests the delays were linked to a change we released this
morning to how the system retries jobs. We have switched that change off
while we confirm exactly what happened. We are also checking affected jobs
carefully before reprocessing any of them, so nothing is run twice by
mistake.

We are sorry for the disruption. We will share a further update once our
review is complete. If you are still seeing delays, please contact support.
