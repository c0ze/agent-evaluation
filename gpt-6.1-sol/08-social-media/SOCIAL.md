# Threadmark — launch day

## LinkedIn post

Research folders have a way of growing faster than our memory of what’s in them. A paper here, a note there, and a sentence we’d like to check against its source.

Today we’re launching Threadmark, a small desktop app that turns folders of research PDFs and notes into a local, searchable evidence map. Processing stays on your computer.

The launch feature is source-linked summaries. Click a sentence to open the supporting passage. You can read the passage, inspect the context, and decide whether it supports the summary. The link keeps the passage close to the summary, so you can move between the two as you read.

Threadmark is available now for Windows and macOS, with a 14-day trial, then $12/month. Linux is being investigated, but we can’t promise a release.

For the moment when “I read that somewhere” needs a more useful answer.
