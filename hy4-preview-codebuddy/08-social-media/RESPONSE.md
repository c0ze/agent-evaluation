# Voice choices

The launch posts lead with the mechanism, not the mood: source-linked summaries,
processed locally, $12 after a 14-day trial. That is the only claim we can
actually stand behind, so it carries the whole announcement — no adoption numbers,
no speed figures, no promised Linux date.

Platform shapes length, not substance. LinkedIn gets room to explain why the
citation link matters. Bluesky gets the same facts in 277 characters. Instagram
gets warmer, shorter sentences and four hashtags.

Every reply answers the specific worry instead of thanking anyone. The chatbot
question gets a contrast, not a denial. Seats get a plain "not yet" plus what
exists today. Training gets an architectural answer. The outage gets
acknowledgement, a next step, and an explicit request not to send account details
or documents. Japanese gets an honest "untested" with a way to check it during the
trial.
