# Voice notes

I kept Threadmark precise, warm, and mildly nerdy, never grandiose. The launch
posts lead with the one concrete feature — source-linked summaries — and show
rather than hype: “click a sentence, the passage opens.” Platform shapes
register: LinkedIn gets fuller sentences and a straight invitation to
critique; Bluesky gets clipped, lowercase honesty; Instagram gets a warmer
visual cadence with restrained hashtags.

The hard limits held. No customer counts, performance claims, certifications,
or roadmap promises appear anywhere; Linux is named as under investigation,
never committed. The outage reply acknowledges the frustration and moves
account-specific details to a private channel, so nothing private surfaces
publicly.

Across the skeptical comments the move is the same: agree with what's fair,
correct what's wrong, and admit what I don't know — because a tool built on
verifiable sources should sound verifiable itself.
