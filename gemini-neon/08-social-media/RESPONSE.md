# Voice choices

Threadmark's launch copy aims to sound precise, warm, mildly nerdy, and grounded in verifiable facts. Instead of grandiose claims, invented metrics, or engagement bait, each post focuses on what the app actually does—turning folders of PDFs and notes into a local evidence map with clickable source passages—framed around everyday research friction like messy Downloads folders and footnotes.

Each post fits its platform: LinkedIn offers structured context, Bluesky stays direct within 280 characters, and Instagram pairs tactile imagery with four restrained hashtags.

The comment replies stay calm, specific, and honest without canned filler. They clarify what Threadmark is not (a chatbot wrapper or training pipeline) and avoid roadmap promises on Linux, lab billing, or Japanese support. For the stuck indexing report, the reply acknowledges the failure directly, offers a concrete next step, and moves support to DM to protect private account details.
