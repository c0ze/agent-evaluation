# Threadmark launch day

## LinkedIn

Threadmark launches today for Windows and macOS. It’s a desktop app that turns a folder of research PDFs and notes into a local, searchable evidence map.

Say you’re drafting a literature review and a summary mentions an effect that showed up in one subgroup. With source-linked summaries, you can click that sentence to open the supporting passage and see how the authors describe the result. That detail matters when you’re deciding what to cite and how to phrase it.

Threadmark processes your files on your computer. Start with the folder you already use for a project, search across your material, and follow a summary sentence back to the passage it draws on.

You can try Threadmark for 14 days. It’s $12/month after the trial.
