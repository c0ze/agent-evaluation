# Threadmark launch day

## LinkedIn

Research folders tend to grow by accretion: PDFs here, notes there, and the passage you need somewhere in between.

Threadmark is a small desktop app that turns folders of research PDFs and notes into a local, searchable evidence map. Its launch feature is source-linked summaries: click a sentence to open the passage supporting it. Your documents are processed on your computer.

Threadmark is available now for Windows and macOS. Try it during the 14-day trial; after that, it’s $12/month. Linux is something we’re investigating, but we don’t have a release promise to share.

A useful test is to take a familiar folder of your own material and see whether you can find the evidence you need, then verify each summary against its source. That’s the job we built Threadmark for.
