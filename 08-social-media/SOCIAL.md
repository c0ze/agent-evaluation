Today we’re launching Threadmark, a desktop app that turns a folder of research PDFs and notes into a searchable evidence map. Its summaries are linked to the source. Click a sentence in a summary and the supporting passage opens, so you can read that “however” in context.

Say you’re writing a literature review and two papers seem to disagree. Open the passage behind each summary sentence and check who was in each study. A difference that matters to your review might be right there in the methods.

Threadmark processes your files on your computer. It’s available today for Windows and macOS, with a 14-day trial and a price of $12/month after that.
