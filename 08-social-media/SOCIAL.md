## LinkedIn post

Today we're launching Threadmark, a desktop app that turns folders of research PDFs and notes into a local, searchable evidence map.

The launch feature is source-linked summaries: click any sentence in a summary and Threadmark opens the supporting passage in the source document. Checking whether a claim holds up is one click instead of one long scroll.

All processing happens on your computer. Your files stay on your machine.

Threadmark runs on Windows and macOS today. Linux is being investigated, and we'd rather not promise a date we can't keep. Pricing is $12/month after a 14-day trial.

If you've ever lost an evening hunting for a passage you'd already read, we built this for you. If you try it, tell us what works and what doesn't.

You're meant to verify against the source, not trust the summary. Processing runs on your machine, so your files aren't uploaded for processing. If it still feels like a wrapper two days into the 14-day trial, tell us — that's what the trial is for.
