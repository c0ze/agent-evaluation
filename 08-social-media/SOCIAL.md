# Threadmark — Launch Day Social

## LinkedIn post

Today we're launching Threadmark, a small desktop app for researchers drowning in PDFs.

Point it at a folder of papers and notes, and it builds a local, searchable evidence map of what you actually have. Our launch feature is source-linked summaries: every summary sentence links back to the passage it came from, so you can click through and check the context yourself.

Everything processes on your computer — your files stay yours. No uploads needed to search, summarize, or trace a claim back to its source.

Threadmark is available now for Windows and macOS at $12/month after a 14-day trial. We're a small team, so we're starting focused: doing one thing carefully rather than promising everything.

If you spend your weeks cross-checking citations or hunting for that one paragraph you know you read, we'd love for you to try it.

## Replies

### 1. LinkedIn — "Is this just another chatbot wrapping my documents?"

Fair question — the difference is that Threadmark shows its work. It maps your PDFs and notes into a searchable evidence map, and every summary sentence links to the supporting passage, so you click through and read the original context. Processing stays on your computer, and the point is tracing claims to sources, not chatting past them.

### 2. LinkedIn — "Can a university buy seats for a lab?"

Right now Threadmark is sold as individual seats at $12/month after a 14-day trial, for Windows and macOS. We don't have a separate lab or university plan to offer today. If you tell us roughly how many seats your lab would need and how you'd want billing to work, it helps us understand the demand while we stay focused on the individual release.

### 3. Bluesky — "no linux, no deal"

Totally fair. We're Windows + macOS only at launch. We're investigating Linux but haven't promised it — we'll say so plainly if that changes.

### 4. Bluesky — "Does local mean you train on my papers anyway?"

No — processing stays on your computer, and your files aren't uploaded for search, summaries, or training. The evidence map lives locally with your documents.

### 5. Instagram — "I paid and the app has been stuck indexing for two hours."

That's frustrating, and I'm sorry — two hours is not normal. Try quitting and reopening Threadmark, then indexing a single small folder first to see if it completes. If it still stalls, send us a DM with your OS version and roughly how many PDFs were in the folder and we'll dig in from there.

### 6. Instagram — "Would this work for a literature review in Japanese?"

Honest answer: we haven't validated Threadmark specifically for Japanese-language literature reviews, so I can't promise how clean the summaries or search will be there. The source links still take you to the original passage either way. The 14-day trial is the best way to test it on your own files and see if it holds up.
