# Threadmark launch day

## LinkedIn post

Today we are launching Threadmark.

Threadmark is a small desktop app for a familiar problem: a folder full of research PDFs and notes, and no clear thread through them. It turns that folder into a local, searchable evidence map.

Our launch feature is source-linked summaries. Click a sentence in a summary, and Threadmark opens the passage that supports it, so you can check the evidence instead of taking a summary on trust. Processing stays on your computer.

Threadmark is available now for Windows and macOS. The trial runs for 14 days; after that, it is $12 a month. Linux is something we are investigating, not something we are promising today.

If your research lives in PDFs you keep reopening to find one passage, we built Threadmark for that desk, that folder, and that very specific sigh.
