Today we are launching Threadmark for Windows and macOS.

Anyone working with a dense folder of research PDFs and reading notes knows the quiet frustration of remembering an argument without remembering which page of which paper held the evidence. You end up with twenty open tabs and a search bar full of guessed keywords.

Point it at a folder of PDFs and notes, and it builds a local, searchable evidence map right on your computer. ## 4. Replies to Sample Comments

### LinkedIn

**Comment:** “Is this just another chatbot wrapping my documents?”  
**Reply:** No—there is no chat prompt or conversational back-and-forth. Threadmark is built for navigation and verification rather than open-ended chat. It indexes your folder of PDFs and notes into a structured evidence map and generates source-linked summaries where every sentence points to the underlying text. Clicking a sentence opens the exact supporting passage in your original document so you can verify the context directly.

**Comment:** “Can a university buy seats for a lab?”  
**Reply:** At launch, we only have self-serve individual subscriptions ($12/month after the 14-day trial) and haven't built centralized multi-seat management or institutional licensing yet. If your lab members can use individual accounts with itemized receipts for reimbursement, that works today. If your department requires a single consolidated invoice for a group of seats, send us an email with your university's purchasing requirements and we will see if we can set that up manually.

### Bluesky

**Comment:** “no linux, no deal”  
**Reply:** Fair. We only have Windows and macOS builds ready today. We're actively investigating what it takes to package and support a Linux build cleanly, though we aren't promising a release until we're sure we can maintain it properly.

**Comment:** “Does local mean you train on my papers anyway?”  
**Reply:** No. We never train on your papers, and we never see them. All indexing, mapping, and summarization happen locally on your computer—your PDFs and notes stay on your drive and are never uploaded to our servers.

### Instagram

**Comment:** “I paid and the app has been stuck indexing for two hours.”  
**Reply:** It shouldn't be hanging like that, especially right after you subscribed. Sometimes a malformed or scanned PDF in a large folder stalls the local indexer. Try quitting the app and pointing it at a smaller subfolder first to see if a specific file is blocking the queue. If it stays stuck, please export the diagnostic log from the Help menu (it doesn't contain your document text) and send it via DM or our support email—please don't post your account email or receipt details here in public comments—and we'll help troubleshoot and make sure your billing period is adjusted.

**Comment:** “Would this work for a literature review in Japanese?”  
**Reply:** We designed and tested Threadmark primarily on English documents for launch, so we don't want to oversell how well it handles Japanese yet. Because CJK word boundaries and vertical two-column academic PDF layouts behave differently during local text extraction and summarization, results on Japanese papers may be uneven. The safest test is to load a representative folder of your Japanese PDFs during the 14-day free trial to see if the text extraction and source links hold up on your specific files before paying anything.
