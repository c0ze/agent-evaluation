# Voice rationale

Threadmark's voice is precise, warm, mildly nerdy, never grandiose — so the posts lead with the verifiable mechanic (every summary sentence links to its source passage) rather than hype, and state limits plainly: Linux is "being investigated, not promised," and Japanese support is untested rather than oversold. No customer counts, benchmarks, certifications, or roadmap dates appear, because none exist in the brand brief.

The replies match each platform's register: LinkedIn gets fuller, professional answers; Bluesky gets short, direct ones; Instagram stays conversational. Skeptical comments get specifics instead of defensiveness ("that's a bug, not a feature"; "local means local"). The outage reply acknowledges the frustration, offers a concrete next step (restart resumes indexing, then Help → Send Diagnostics), and steers account details to private channels. No engagement bait, no canned "thanks for your feedback."
