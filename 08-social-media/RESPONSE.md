# Voice choices

LinkedIn opens with the reader's problem (juggling PDFs) before the feature, mirroring how researchers describe their own work, and ends on a concrete action (the trial) instead of a hype line.

Bluesky stays dense and slightly technical ("source-linked summaries") — that audience values precision over emojis, and the limit rewards brevity.

Instagram leads with a relatable image (paper-graveyard Downloads folder) and one emoji, then keeps the feature explainer factual.

Each reply does one job: clarify, admit uncertainty, or offer a concrete next step. The outage reply acknowledges the issue, gives a restart plus a log path, and skips "thanks for reaching out." The Linux reply stays honest about not having a date.
