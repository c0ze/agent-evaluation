# Voice choices

Every claim stays inside BRAND.md: local processing, source-linked summaries, $12 after a 14-day trial, Windows and macOS, Linux under investigation. Nothing about speed, accuracy, user numbers, certifications, or dates.

Warmth comes from plain sentences and admitting limits, not exclamation marks. The mild nerdiness lives in small images ("pointers you can check", "chatbot in a trench coat"), never in jargon.

Each platform shifts register: LinkedIn is explanatory and full-sentence; Bluesky is compressed and direct; Instagram is shorter, contractions on, link in bio, four hashtags.

Replies answer the actual question first, then give one concrete next step. The Linux and university replies say what does not exist instead of hinting. The outage reply apologises, moves diagnostics to DM, and asks the customer to keep payment details out of the comments. The Japanese reply refuses to guess and points at the trial.
