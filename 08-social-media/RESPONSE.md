# Voice choices

**Claims trace to the brand doc.** Local processing, source-linked summaries, $12 after
14 days, Windows and macOS now, Linux explicitly not promised. No customer counts, no
speed claims, no adjectives doing work the evidence can't do.

**Honesty is load-bearing, not a disclaimer.** The Linux line, "no institutional
licensing," and the untested-Japanese answer are the parts I'd defend. Naming a gap costs
less than getting caught overstating one.

**Warm, not breezy.** Short sentences, concrete nouns, one mildly nerdy aside (the lost
highlight). The reader already knows what an embedding is.

**Replies answer the real worry.** Each takes the actual objection — privacy,
procurement, platform, training, a stuck app, a language — and gives a specific next
step. No "thanks for your feedback," no question-bait. The outage reply routes people to
in-app support, because logs are the only real diagnostic available and account details
don't belong in a public thread.
