# Voice choices

- **Facts only from the brief.** Every claim traces to BRAND.md. Where the brief is silent (Japanese support, lab licensing, data beyond indexing), the replies say so instead of guessing.
- **Precise, not grandiose.** The source-linked summary carries the pitch. No superlatives, percentages, certifications, or dates.
- **Short, specific apologies.** The outage reply names the problem, owns the wait, and moves account and payment details to DMs.
- **Calm on pushback.** The Linux and data replies state the situation and stop. They neither argue nor reassure.
- **Mildly nerdy.** Concrete nouns ("passage behind the sentence", "local, searchable evidence map") do the work that adjectives would.
- **Platform fit.** LinkedIn gets full sentences, Bluesky stays under 280 characters, Instagram stays brief.
- **No bait or templates.** No calls to comment and no "thanks for your feedback."
