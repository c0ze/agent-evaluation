# Voice choices

I built every post around the one concrete thing Threadmark does: click a sentence, see the supporting passage. That keeps the voice precise and mildly nerdy ("show its work") without grand claims. Only facts from BRAND.md appear: local processing, the $12/month price after a 14-day trial, and Windows and macOS. Linux is described as investigated, not promised.

Length and register follow each platform: LinkedIn is fuller and professional, Bluesky is compact and plain, Instagram is warmer. No post ends with a comment prompt.

In replies, the brief gave no facts on university seats or Japanese support, so I say so and suggest a way to find out, rather than guess. The outage reply apologizes, asks for non-sensitive diagnostics, and moves account details to a private message. I avoided stock thank-you phrasing and refund or fix promises.
