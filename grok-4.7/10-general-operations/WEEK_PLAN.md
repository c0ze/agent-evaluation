# Week plan — Monday to Friday

Hours are 09:30–17:30, Monday to Friday. Wednesday 13:00–17:30 is closed. Nothing in this plan sits outside those hours. Lunch is 45 minutes every day. Meeting load stays under the four-hour daily preference. Where someone else's availability was not stated, the time is marked as a hold or an ask, not as a booking.

## What wins when two things want the same hour

1. Board numbers, once Lina's corrections are in hand, through the Thursday 12:00 final.
2. The renewals escalation, answered on Monday. It has no later day.
3. Security-patch review on Monday, or on Tuesday if Monday cannot hold it, then QA and a 30-minute change window in time for a Friday 16:00 deploy.
4. The other two support escalations, both done by Wednesday 12:15 (the last open block before lunch and the Wednesday afternoon close).
5. Demo rehearsal with Sam, Wednesday 10:00–11:30, unless the board block has no earlier home.
6. Strategy outline sent by Tuesday 14:00, so the 16:00 leadership meeting can review it.
7. Customer demo hold, Thursday 16:00–17:30.

The strategy draft and the live demo move before the hard deadlines. The rehearsal moves only when the board block has no earlier home.

## Dependencies

1. Lina's Monday 15:00 update decides where the board block lives. The two hours cannot start before her corrections arrive.
2. Board path: corrections, then two uninterrupted hours, then numbers final Thursday 12:00. Finance described those two hours as the work after a Tuesday-morning delivery.
3. Patch path: review (60–90 min), then QA for half a day, then a 30-minute change window, deployed by Friday 16:00. QA's half day has to follow the review. QA cannot do Thursday.
4. The review is planned for Monday, with Tuesday as the backup. Wednesday's default morning is the rehearsal plus two short gaps, and the board-slip morning is the two-hour finance block, so neither leaves 60–90 minutes. A Friday review is not in the plan: QA would have to start as soon as the review ended, and a half day plus the 30-minute window would have to finish by 16:00 with almost no slack. QA's own hours were not stated, so that back-to-back Friday is not something to count on.
5. Strategy path: three focused hours, then a send by Tuesday 14:00, then an optional review in the Tuesday 16:00 meeting. Two of those hours have to happen Monday afternoon. Tuesday morning before 11:00 is only 90 minutes, and from 11:00 the default week belongs to the board numbers. Monday afternoon is what makes the Tuesday review possible.
6. Rehearsal path: Sam is out Tuesday. His only stated window is Wednesday 10:00–12:00. The rehearsal is 90 minutes and he must join, so it is Wednesday 10:00–11:30.
7. Demo path: Wednesday 15:00 is inside the closed afternoon. The remaining offered slot is Thursday 16:00. That hold still depends on the customer confirming, on the length fitting before 17:30, and on Sam if he is required live. His Thursday availability was not stated.
8. Support path: the renewals item is Monday-only. Wednesday can absorb one later escalation (11:30–12:15) and not two. One Wednesday-SLA item is on Monday, the other on Tuesday, with Wednesday as the backup for the Tuesday one.
