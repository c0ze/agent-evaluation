The story moves Mara from fearful denial — treating the future message as a glitch or a threat — through reluctant curiosity to a quiet recognition that the voice is her own from the past, reframing the message from warning to welcome. The emotional arc is one of learning to stop bracing for loss and to accept being cared for, even by oneself across time. It closes not on dread of the rising sea but on the small, steady courage of leaving a light on, suggesting that memory and preservation are acts of love rather than grief.
