The intended arc moves Mara from braced, solitary control — preserving everyone else's words while rehearsing her own — through fear that the future confirms her estrangement from Tomas, to a quieter acceptance that connection can arrive unscripted. His ordinary, practical help turns the future message from a warning into reassurance, allowing her to risk imperfect honesty in person rather than send a polished recording. She ends neither healed nor rescued, but less defended, able to leave both a message unsent and the salt uncleaned.
