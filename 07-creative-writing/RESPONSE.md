The story starts in Tamsin's procedural calm and moves to a quiet dread when she hears what seems to be her own older voice, alone in the flooded house and telling someone not to come. That dread turns to grief and an incomplete absolution when the salt line on the third stair shows the message is her mother's, recorded during the surge ten years ago and never sent. A prophecy that she will stay becomes permission to leave, clouded by the question of why her mother kept it back. It ends on a release she has not finished, with the pen stopped at the forwarding address.
