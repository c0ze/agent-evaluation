# Threadmark launch day

## LinkedIn post

Today we're launching Threadmark, a small desktop app for research folders.

Point it at a folder of PDFs and notes, and it builds a local, searchable evidence map of what you've collected. The launch feature is source-linked summaries. Click any sentence in a summary and it opens the passage that supports it, so you can check the claim against the source instead of trusting the wording.

That check is the point. A summary you can't verify is just a confident guess, and we'd rather you verify it than take our word for it.

Processing stays on your computer. Threadmark runs on Windows and macOS today. Linux is being investigated, but we aren't promising it yet.

It costs $12 a month after a 14-day trial. If you have a literature review sitting in a folder, the trial is a good way to test it on your own papers.
