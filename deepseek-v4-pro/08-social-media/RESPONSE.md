# Voice choices

Precise: we state exactly what Threadmark does (source-linked summaries, local processing) and name the price, trial, and platforms rather than leaning on adjectives.

Warm: launch posts speak directly to researchers and end with an invitation, not a slogan.

Mildly nerdy, never grandiose: we call the product what it is — a searchable evidence map — and avoid words like "revolutionary" or "AI-powered."

Honesty above optimism: Linux is "under investigation," not "coming soon." Language support is unvalidated, not implied. Seating/licensing is absent, so we say so and ask what a lab needs instead of inventing a plan.

Replies mirror the platform: short and direct on Bluesky, more explanatory on LinkedIn, practical and private on Instagram. The outage reply acknowledges the problem, gives a concrete next step, and routes details off the public thread.
