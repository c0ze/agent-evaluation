## Comment replies

### LinkedIn — "Is this just another chatbot wrapping my documents?"

It isn't — Threadmark doesn't wrap a language model around your documents. There's no chat box and no generated answers. It reads your files on your machine and writes source-linked summaries: every sentence opens the specific passage it came from, so nothing is paraphrased without its citation. If a claim isn't in your corpus, there's no place for it to come from.

### LinkedIn — "Can a university buy seats for a lab?"

Campus or lab licensing isn't something we offer yet. Right now Threadmark is sold as individual subscriptions — $12/month after a 14-day trial — with no seat-based or multi-user purchasing. If you can tell us what a lab deal would need (how many seats, admin billing, access controls), we'll factor that in as we figure out whether team plans make sense. No timeline we can promise today.

### Bluesky — "no linux, no deal"

Understood. Linux is genuinely being investigated, but we won't promise it until it's ready. If it ships, we'll announce it plainly — no countdowns, no waitlists.

### Bluesky — "Does local mean you train on my papers anyway?"

No. "Local" means all processing happens on your computer and your PDFs don't leave it to run Threadmark. We don't train anything on your papers, and the summaries are produced from your own documents, not learned from them.

### Instagram — "I paid and the app has been stuck indexing for two hours."

That isn't how indexing should behave, and we're sorry you hit it. A good first step: quit the app, reopen it, and try indexing one small folder on its own to see if it still stalls. If it does, please reach out to us through the app's support link so we can look at the logs on your machine — we won't ask you to share account or billing details here.

### Instagram — "Would this work for a literature review in Japanese?"

We've built and tested Threadmark around English-language sources, so we can't promise how it will handle Japanese. Search and summarization are tuned for English, and Japanese support isn't something we've validated. If you run the 14-day trial on Japanese PDFs, we'd genuinely like to know what works and where it falters.
