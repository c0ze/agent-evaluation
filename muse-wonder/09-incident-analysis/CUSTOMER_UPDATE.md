# Service update — delays in our European region

Earlier today, some jobs and checkouts in our European region were delayed
while a backlog built up in our processing queue. The backlog has now
cleared, and services are operating normally.

The delays began shortly after a software update that changed how we retry
failed jobs for individual customers. Our early findings suggest this change
did not behave as expected for some accounts, which contributed to the
backlog. We have turned that change off and reversed the update, and
processing recovered after we did so. Our investigation is continuing, and
we have not yet confirmed every detail of what happened.

We are checking jobs from the affected period to confirm they completed as
expected, and we will contact any customers whose work needs attention. We
are also adding safeguards before anything similar is reintroduced.

We are sorry for the disruption and appreciate your patience.
