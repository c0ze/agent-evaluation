# Threadmark — launch day social

## LinkedIn post

Meet Threadmark, a small desktop app for people who live in folders of PDFs.

Threadmark turns your research PDFs and notes into a local, searchable evidence map. Its new source-linked summaries are the part we are most proud of: click a sentence in a summary, and Threadmark opens the passage that supports it, so you can check the evidence instead of taking the summary on trust.

Everything is processed on your own computer, not sent away for processing. You choose the folders, build the map, and follow each claim back to its source — useful when you are writing, reviewing, or trying to remember why you highlighted something three months ago.

Threadmark is available now for Windows and macOS. It costs $12 a month after a 14-day trial.

Small tool, specific job: keep your evidence close, and make it findable again.

## Replies

### LinkedIn — “Is this just another chatbot wrapping my documents?”

Fair question — no. A chatbot answers from your documents; Threadmark maps them. It builds a local, searchable evidence map from your PDFs and notes, and its summaries are source-linked: click a sentence and you see the exact passage behind it. Processing stays on your computer, so you can verify claims against the original text rather than trusting a generated answer.

### LinkedIn — “Can a university buy seats for a lab?”

At launch we sell individual subscriptions — $12 a month after a 14-day trial — and we do not have a university or lab seat plan to announce today. If you message us here with roughly how many people are in the lab, we can reply directly about whether there is a workable route for your setup, without promising one in advance.

### Bluesky — “no linux, no deal”

Understood. Threadmark is Windows and macOS today. Linux is something we are investigating, but we are not promising it — so if Linux is essential, it makes sense to wait and see.

### Bluesky — “Does local mean you train on my papers anyway?”

No. Local means processing stays on your computer. Threadmark builds its evidence map from your PDFs and notes on your machine; we do not train on your papers.

### Instagram — “I paid and the app has been stuck indexing for two hours.”

Sorry — two hours stuck is not how this should feel, especially on a paid account. Please quit and reopen Threadmark, then try indexing a smaller folder first to see if it completes. If it hangs again, send us a DM here with your OS version and roughly how many files are in the folder — no account details or documents needed in the message — and we will help you work through it.

### Instagram — “Would this work for a literature review in Japanese?”

We have not validated Japanese-language reviews well enough to promise that, so we do not want to guess. The safest test is the 14-day trial: add a small folder of your Japanese PDFs and notes, then check whether search finds what you expect and whether source links open the right passages. That will tell you more than we can.
