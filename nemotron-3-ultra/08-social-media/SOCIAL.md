## Comment Replies

### LinkedIn: "Is this just another chatbot wrapping my documents?"

No — there's no chatbot. Threadmark extracts and summarizes, then links each sentence to the passage it came from. You read the summary; if you want the context, click and the PDF opens at that page. No generation, just retrieval and compression.

### LinkedIn: "Can a university buy seats for a lab?"

Not yet — current licensing is per-user via the website. Volume or institutional options aren't built out. Happy to discuss requirements if you email support@threadmark.app.

### Bluesky: "no linux, no deal"

Understood. Linux is being investigated but I'm not promising a timeline. If you're on Linux and want to be notified when there's something concrete, there's a "notify me" field on the site.

### Bluesky: "Does local mean you train on my papers anyway?"

No training, no upload, no telemetry on document content. The app indexes and summarizes entirely on your machine. The only network call is the license check at startup.

### Instagram: "I paid and the app has been stuck indexing for two hours."

That shouldn't happen — sorry. First, try restarting the app; indexing resumes where it left off. If it's still stuck, email support@threadmark.app with your OS and rough folder size and I'll help diagnose. No account details needed.

### Instagram: "Would this work for a literature review in Japanese?"

Yes, if the PDFs contain selectable text (not scanned images). The summarization handles Japanese, and source linking works the same way. OCR for scanned pages isn't built in yet.
