Water

Margin follows an issue’s theme into ground most maps leave out. This time the theme is what remains when the water goes down: a riverbed, a night route, an unfinished tower, a bath with no swimmers.

Cover story

The River Keeps Its Receipts

by Imani Vale

Along a drought-exposed riverbed, shop ledgers, bottle caps, and vanished street plans surface together. A field essay about civic memory and what infrastructure tries to forget.
