The River Keeps Its Receipts

Along a drought-exposed riverbed, shop ledgers, bottle caps, and vanished street plans surface together. A field essay about civic memory and what infrastructure tries to forget.

Imani Vale Field essay·28 pages

In this piece

  • i Ledger fragments recovered from four shuttered shops, 2019–2024.
  • ii A street plan drawn twice, thirty-one years apart.
  • iii What the water returns, and in what order.

Photographs by the author. Survey data courtesy of the regional water office.
