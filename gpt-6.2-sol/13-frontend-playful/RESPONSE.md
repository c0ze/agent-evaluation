# Moss

Moss feels like a small plant-care desk. Cream paper, a chamomile banner, and a hand-drawn fern set a warm tone. Each plant has its own little portrait, from Dot’s speckled pads to Drama’s striped leaves. A terracotta badge and a tinted first row draw attention to overdue care. On a wide screen, the weekly rhythm sits beside the queue. On a phone, the queue comes first and each action has room to tap.

Open `index.html` directly to use the app offline. The queue starts with overdue care, followed by today’s tasks and a separate preview of what’s next. A plant’s name expands its light, last watering, and observation history. Saving care moves the plant to Completed care and adds a dated history entry. Snooze moves the task to the next local calendar day. The confirmation offers nine seconds to undo the latest action, with the timer paused while the confirmation has focus or the pointer is over it. When the queue is clear, Moss shows which plant is due next or points you to the saved care notes.

The browser saves care and snoozes locally when storage is available. The supplied relative due dates are anchored to the first visit. The weekly chart shows the supplied Monday-to-Sunday rhythm and marks the current weekday.

The interface uses native buttons, a skip link, clear focus outlines, and labeled expandable regions. Keyboard activation of a care action puts focus on Undo, and undoing returns focus to the restored action. A live region announces changes. Due dates and chart values have text labels, and the current weekday is also underlined. Reduced-motion preferences disable the chevron transition.
