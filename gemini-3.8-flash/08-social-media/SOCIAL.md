# Social Media Launch Content

## 1. LinkedIn Post

Today we are releasing Threadmark, a desktop app designed to help researchers organize literature without losing track of original sources.

If you work with dense folders of academic papers and notes, you know the frustration of summaries that cannot be easily verified. Threadmark builds a local, searchable evidence map from your documents. Its core launch feature is source-linked summaries: every generated sentence links directly to the exact passage supporting it, so clicking an insight opens the underlying text in your PDF or note.

All processing stays strictly on your computer. Your files and notes never leave your machine or get sent to remote servers.

Threadmark is available now for macOS and Windows. You can try it with a 14-day trial, after which it costs $12 per month. If you spend significant time corroborating claims across research papers, we hope it makes your reading and synthesis feel much more grounded.

## 4. Replies to Sample Comments

### LinkedIn Comment 1
> "Is this just another chatbot wrapping my documents?"

**Reply:**
No, Threadmark is built quite differently from a document chat wrapper. Instead of conversational Q&A, it generates an evidence map with source-linked summaries directly from your PDFs and notes. Its key utility is citation fidelity: clicking any sentence in a summary opens and highlights the exact supporting passage in your source document. Because all processing stays locally on your machine, your research stays private and verified rather than passing through a conversational prompt.
