# Monday–Friday operating plan

Work hours are 09:30–17:30. Wednesday ends at 13:00. Each day includes 45 minutes for lunch. The listed meetings fit within the preferred four-hour daily cap. QA, rehearsal, demo, and deployment times are proposed holds pending confirmation.

**Hard deadlines** are the renewal escalation response on Monday, the two remaining support SLAs on Wednesday, final board numbers by Thursday 12:00, and patch deployment by Friday 16:00. Support should confirm the Wednesday SLA cutoff times on Monday.

**Preferred timing** is an early-week strategy outline and a Wednesday demo. Tuesday 14:00 is the send-by cutoff for review at the 16:00 leadership meeting. Thursday 16:00 is the customer’s alternate demo time.
