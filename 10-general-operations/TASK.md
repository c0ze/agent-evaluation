# Task 10 — General agent work: synthesize a messy operating week

Read `INBOX.md`. Produce:

- `WEEK_PLAN.md`: prioritized plan for Monday–Friday with dependencies, focus
  blocks, deadlines, and contingency space.
- `DECISIONS.md`: confirmed decisions, unresolved contradictions, and questions
  assigned to the right person.
- `DRAFT_REPLY.md`: one concise message to the team aligning expectations and
  surfacing the decisions needed. It should sound human and avoid overpromising.

Respect stated working hours and do not silently invent availability. Distinguish
hard deadlines from preferences. Write `RESPONSE.md` with the reasoning behind
your top three priorities. Work only in this directory.
