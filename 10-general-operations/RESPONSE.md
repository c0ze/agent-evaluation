# Reasoning Behind the Top Three Priorities

I ranked by how likely each item is to miss a hard deadline and how bad a miss would be. I didn't rank by who asked or who is loudest. Sequence is different from priority: the renewal escalation goes first on Monday because it's short and due that day, but it's low-risk once it's scheduled.

## 1. Security patch review, Monday morning
- **Hard deadline** (Fri 16:00) and the **longest chain**: my review (up to 90 min), then half a day of QA, then a 30-minute change window.
- **QA is out Thursday.** A review done after Wednesday would push QA to Friday, and a single defect would then blow the deadline. Reviewing Monday gives QA Mon/Tue/Wed for the first pass and keeps Friday as a real fallback.
- It's also the item I control fully. Nothing blocks me from starting, so a delay would be purely my own.

## 2. Board numbers: protect Tue 11:00–13:00 and settle the fallback on Monday
- **Hard deadline** (Thu 12:00) that depends on someone else's upstream work (vendor export, then Lina).
- The risk shows up Monday 15:00, but it costs the most on Thursday. If the corrections slip past Tuesday afternoon, my calendar has only one more 2-hour slot, Thu 09:30–11:30, with a 30-minute margin. Wednesday's short day and the rehearsal break up the rest of the week.
- So it's priority #2 even though the work happens Tuesday. The real action is getting Finance's call on the fallback **on Monday**, while there's still room to change the approach.

## 3. Resolve the demo conflict and lock the rehearsal with Sam
- The demo time is a **preference**, but it currently **conflicts with my stated unavailability** (Wed 13:00–17:30). I haven't quietly accepted a slot I can't make, and I haven't quietly moved it either. Product owns the call, so I surfaced it.
- The rehearsal is the fragile part. Sam's only confirmed window is Wed 10:00–12:00, and his other days are unknown. The 90-minute rehearsal has to happen there whichever demo date wins, so I'm asking him to hold it now.
- It's a customer-facing commitment, and a Thu 16:00 booking gets harder to secure the longer we wait.

## Why the others ranked lower
- **CEO outline:** "early this week" is a preference, and the only real cutoff is Tue 14:00. Three focused hours Monday afternoon cover it with a day of slack, so it's important but low-risk once scheduled.
- **Escalations:** the renewal one is time-bound and handled Monday. I moved the two Wednesday-SLA ones to Tuesday because Wednesday is a half day with an unknown SLA time. That's about 2.25 h in total, scheduled and low-risk.

## Constraints I held to
- I only scheduled my own time inside 09:30–17:30, and nothing on Wednesday after 13:00.
- Lunch is at least 45 min every day. Meetings peak at about 2 h/day (Thursday if the demo moves), well under the 4 h cap.
- I didn't assume anyone else's availability (QA's hours, Sam outside Wednesday, the change-window owner). Each gap is a named question in DECISIONS.md.
