# Why these three

This is the order I keep when two blocks want the same hour. An earlier clock does not automatically win. The renewals answer is due Monday and takes 45 free minutes, so it is easy to keep. The board numbers are due later and have almost no legal window once Wednesday afternoon is closed and the vendor export might slip. The patch is due Friday, and the review still has to land by Tuesday or the QA chain gets fragile. The notes give no impact ranking among the three, so the order is about slack and collisions. Under them sit the strategy outline ("early this week," and on the Tuesday 16:00 agenda only if sent by 14:00) and the customer demo (Wednesday 15:00 preferred, Thursday 16:00 also offered).

The Monday calendar still does the renewals answer first and the patch review second, because on a normal Monday both fit and the renewal has to leave the same day. The sections below are the collision order.

## 1. Board numbers, final Thursday 12:00

This is the commitment with the least slack. Finance needs two uninterrupted hours after Lina's corrections, and the numbers must be final Thursday at 12:00. Lina's forecast is Tuesday by 11:00, with an explicit chance the vendor export is late, and she will know by Monday 15:00.

The backup space is thinner than it looks. Wednesday 13:00–17:30 is closed. The Wednesday morning that remains is a 30-minute gap, a 90-minute rehearsal with Sam, and a 45-minute gap. That is not a two-hour block unless the rehearsal is given up, and only if the corrections are already waiting at 09:30 so lunch can still happen before 13:00. The last window that honors the deadline without touching the rehearsal is Thursday 10:00–12:00, after the standing 09:30 meeting, and that window ends at the deadline. There is no review gap left in it.

So the plan does three things that other work is not allowed to undo. Tuesday 11:00–13:00 stays empty of anything else on the default week, and if the corrections are already in at 09:30 the two hours move to the front of Tuesday ahead of the strategy send. Thursday 10:00–12:00 stays unbooked until Finance releases it. If Tuesday 14:00 passes with no corrections, Wednesday morning is warned as the next home for the block, and the rehearsal yields only at 09:30 Wednesday if the file is actually there. If it is still not there Thursday at 10:00, the noon final gets escalated instead of being promised.

Lina's forecast puts the corrections on Tuesday, so Monday does not contain the two-hour block. Monday's job for this priority is the 15:30 read of her status and a free hour after it to write Finance and Lina if the export is late. A surprise file at 15:30 Monday would still leave only two hours before 17:30, on top of an escalation that also has to go out, so the plan does not count on starting early.

## 2. Renewals escalation, answered Monday

This is the only item that cannot move to another day. The notes ask for a response Monday, call out that it affects renewals, and size it at about 45 minutes with no dependency on Lina, Sam, or QA. It is placed at 10:30–11:15, after the standing half-hour meeting, so a 45-minute answer is one uninterrupted block.

It ranks above the patch review for Monday occupancy and below the board numbers overall. It does not compete with the board block: that block cannot start until Tuesday at the earliest. It does compete with the review if Monday morning collapses. In that case the renewal stays Monday, because Tuesday is not a legal day for it, and the review moves to Tuesday morning, which is still a legal day for the review. The renewal is small enough that this collision is unlikely. It is still the rule.

The other two escalations are real Wednesday SLAs and are scheduled Monday and Tuesday for that reason. They are not in the top three. Wednesday can hold only one of them (11:30–12:15) before lunch and the afternoon close, which is why they are not left until Wednesday, and also why they can slip a day inside the week without becoming the thing the week is built around.

## 3. Security patch, deployed Friday 16:00

The deadline is later than the board's, and the user's own work is smaller: a 60–90 minute review. The risk is the chain, not the size. QA needs half a day after the review, QA cannot work Thursday, and a 30-minute change window has to finish by Friday 16:00.

The review needs a morning that is actually open. On a normal Wednesday the morning is Sam's rehearsal plus a 30-minute gap and a 45-minute gap, and the afternoon is closed. On the board-slip Wednesday the morning is the two-hour finance block. Friday can be made to fit only as a back-to-back review, QA half day, and change window ending by 16:00, and only if QA can start the moment the review ends. QA's hours were not stated, so Friday is not a backup I am willing to plan on. The review is Monday 11:15–12:45, or Tuesday if Monday cannot finish it.

Tuesday is shared with the board block, which is why this ranks under the board numbers. If corrections are already waiting at Tuesday 09:30 and the review has also slipped, the two board hours take 09:30–11:30 and the review takes 12:15–13:45. QA then uses Friday morning rather than a wounded Tuesday afternoon. The Friday 15:00 change window still stands. The strategy send is what misses the 14:00 cutoff in that double slip.

QA's Tuesday half day and the Friday change window are asks. The notes say QA is out Thursday and do not say which other day they will take, or whether the user has to sit in the change window. The plan keeps the user reachable at 15:00–16:00 Friday and does not pretend those bookings are confirmed.

## What sits under these three

The rehearsal is Wednesday 10:00–11:30 because that is the only Sam window in the notes and he has to join. It yields only to the board block, and only under the Wednesday 09:30 rule above. The demo itself is a hold on Thursday 16:00, the sole remaining offered slot, pending the customer, the length, and Sam if he is required live. Wednesday 15:00 is not a near miss. It is inside unavailable time.

The strategy draft is three hours and it is worth doing early. It is also the first large block to shrink. Two of the three hours have to land on Monday afternoon if the Tuesday meeting is going to see a full draft, because Tuesday from 11:00 is the board block on a normal week. If the renewal or the review overruns, leadership meets without the outline. That is a missed preference, and the week is built to allow it.
