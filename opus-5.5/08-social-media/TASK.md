# Task 08 — Social media launch and responses

Use `BRAND.md` to create `SOCIAL.md` for launch day. Deliver:

1. One LinkedIn post (120–180 words).
2. One Bluesky post (maximum 280 characters).
3. One Instagram caption (80–130 words, no more than four hashtags).
4. Replies to all six sample comments. Each reply must fit the platform and be
   specific, calm, and honest.

Do not invent customer counts, performance claims, certifications, or roadmap
commitments. Avoid engagement bait and canned “thanks for your feedback” prose.
The outage comment needs acknowledgement and a useful next step without exposing
private account details.

Write `RESPONSE.md` explaining the voice choices in at most 150 words. Work only
in this directory.
