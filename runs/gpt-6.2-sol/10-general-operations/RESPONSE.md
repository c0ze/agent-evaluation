# Why these are the top three priorities

1. **Respond to the renewal escalation Monday.** It has the earliest hard deadline and affects renewals. A 45-minute morning block leaves the rest of Monday to follow up if the initial response needs input. Handling the two Wednesday cases early also reduces pressure on the short Wednesday.
2. **Protect two uninterrupted hours for the board numbers.** Lina’s corrections are a prerequisite, so Monday’s vendor update determines how much schedule risk Finance faces. Tuesday has two possible starts. Thursday 10:00–12:00 is the final usable block because Wednesday’s rehearsal and afternoon absence rule out a two-hour block.
3. **Start the patch review Monday.** Review, QA, and deployment must run in order. Reviewing Monday gives QA a chance to finish on Tuesday, with Wednesday available if scheduling or fixes push it back. Thursday has no QA coverage. A Friday 14:00 change window leaves 90 minutes before the deadline for verification or recovery.
