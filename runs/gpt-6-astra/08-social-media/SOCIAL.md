# Threadmark launch day

## LinkedIn post

A useful research summary should come with a way to check it.

Today we’re launching Threadmark, a small desktop app that turns folders of research PDFs and notes into a local, searchable evidence map.

The launch feature we’re especially pleased to share: source-linked summaries. Click a sentence to open the supporting passage. You can read the source in context and decide whether the summary holds up. For anyone who keeps asking “where did that come from?”, that connection is the point.

Processing stays on your computer. Your own reading and judgment still matter: a linked passage is a starting point for checking the evidence.

Threadmark is available now for Windows and macOS, with a 14-day trial and a price of $12/month afterward. Try it with a folder you know well, and follow a few sentences back to their sources.
