# Task 07 — Creative writing

Write `STORY.md`, a polished 800–1,100 word literary science-fiction story.

Premise: In a near-future coastal town that is being abandoned to the sea, an
archivist digitizes residents' unsent voice messages. One message appears to be
from the archivist ten years in the future.

Constraints:

- Use close third person and keep the story grounded in physical detail.
- The recurring motif is salt crystallizing on electronics.
- Include one brief moment of dry humor.
- The future message must change meaning by the end without relying on “it was
  all a dream,” a simulation reveal, or a villain monologue.
- Favor subtext over explanation and avoid generic opening-weather exposition.
- Give the story a title.

Write `RESPONSE.md` containing only a 2–4 sentence note about the intended
emotional arc. Work only in this directory.
