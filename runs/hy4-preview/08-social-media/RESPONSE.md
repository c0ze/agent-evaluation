# Voice notes

Launch copy leads with the problem, not the category. "Evidence map" and
"source-linked" carry the whole pitch, so the posts explain one mechanism — click
a sentence, see the passage — instead of promising transformation.

Claims stay narrow: what the app does, what it costs, what ships today. No user
numbers, no speed claims, no certifications. Linux is named as investigated, not
promised, and the university reply scopes before quoting anything.

Replies answer the actual worry. The chatbot question gets the real distinction —
no chat, untraceable lines are withheld. The outage gets acknowledgement, a
private channel, and a factual next step, never account details. The Japanese
question gets an honest "untuned" caveat rather than an easy yes.

Length and shape follow the room: Bluesky is plain and lowercase, LinkedIn is
prose, Instagram is short lines with four hashtags.
