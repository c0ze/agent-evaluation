# SOCIAL.md — Threadmark launch day

## 1. LinkedIn post

Today we're launching Threadmark.

If you've ever stared at a folder of research PDFs and lost track of which paper said what, Threadmark is for you. It's a small desktop app that turns folders of PDFs and notes into a local, searchable evidence map. Every summary is source-linked: click a sentence and the app opens the exact passage it came from. No re-reading forty pages to check one claim.

Your files never leave your computer. Processing happens locally, and your documents are never uploaded or used for training.

Threadmark is $12/month after a 14-day free trial. Windows and macOS are available today. We're investigating a Linux build, but we're not promising one yet.

We built this for researchers, journalists, and anyone who works with a lot of documents and needs to trust where every sentence came from. If that's you, give it a try.

What would make an evidence map genuinely useful in your work?

## 4. Replies to sample comments

**LinkedIn — "Is this just another chatbot wrapping my documents?"**

Fair question. There's no chatbot here. Threadmark reads your files on your machine and builds an evidence map: every summary sentence links back to the exact passage it came from, so you can click through and verify each claim yourself. Nothing is sent to a server, and your documents are never used for training. If you try it and it still feels like a wrapper, tell us why — that's genuinely useful feedback.

**LinkedIn — "Can a university buy seats for a lab?"**

Yes. We can set up lab or department licenses and invoice the university directly. Reach out through the contact form on our site with roughly how many seats you need, and we'll send a quote the same week. Individual licenses are $12/month per person after the 14-day trial.

**Bluesky — "no linux, no deal"**

Fair, and we respect that. Linux is being investigated but not promised — we'd rather ship a build we can actually support than announce early. We'll post here as soon as there's real progress.

**Bluesky — "Does local mean you train on my papers anyway?"**

No. Local means local: processing runs entirely on your machine, files are never uploaded, and nothing you do is used for training — ours or anyone else's. The app even works with no internet connection.

**Instagram — "I paid and the app has been stuck indexing for two hours."**

Sorry — two hours stuck is not the experience we want. A few things to try: large folders take a while, and scanned/image-only PDFs take much longer than text PDFs. Quitting and reopening the app resumes indexing where it left off, so you won't lose progress. If it's still stuck after that, DM us your OS and roughly how many files you're indexing — no account details needed — and we'll dig in.

**Instagram — "Would this work for a literature review in Japanese?"**

Yes. It handles Japanese text, and summaries come out in Japanese when your sources are Japanese. Search also works across folders that mix Japanese and English papers. If a specific PDF gives you trouble, send us a sample and we'll take a look.
