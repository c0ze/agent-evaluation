# RESPONSE.md — Voice choices

We kept the brand voice — precise, warm, mildly nerdy, never grandiose — so every post leads with what the product actually does (a local, source-linked evidence map) rather than hype. We state the price and trial plainly, describe Linux as "being investigated, not promised," and make no customer-count, performance, or certification claims, because there are none to make yet.

The replies are specific, calm, and honest: we admit indexing can be slow, confirm that local means no training, and don't overpromise on Linux. The stuck-indexing reply acknowledges the frustration, offers concrete next steps (reopen the app to resume, check for scanned PDFs), and asks only for OS and approximate file count — never private account details. We avoid engagement bait and canned "thanks for your feedback" prose; each reply answers the actual question and invites a real follow-up.
