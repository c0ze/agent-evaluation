# Voice choices

Threadmark's voice is precise, warm, and mildly nerdy, so every post leads with what the app concretely does: a local evidence map, source-linked summaries. We cite no customer counts, performance numbers, or certifications, because we have none to cite.

Platform shapes the copy. LinkedIn gets complete sentences and the plain facts: price, platforms, Linux status. Bluesky gets the same facts compressed and casual, under 280 characters. Instagram gets the warmest caption and only four hashtags, so the product does the talking.

Replies follow one rule: answer what was actually asked, then say what we can't promise. The Linux and lab-licensing replies say “not today” instead of hedging; the training question gets a factual “no” grounded in local processing; the Japanese question gets honesty over a sales pitch. The outage reply apologizes, gives a diagnostic step, and moves account details to DMs.
