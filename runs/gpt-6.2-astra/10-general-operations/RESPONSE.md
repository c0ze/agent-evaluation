# Why these three come first

1. **Board numbers.** Thursday noon is fixed, and the two hours of work can only start once Lina sends corrections. Her Monday 15:00 update gives us time to respond to an export delay. Protecting Tuesday morning and an afternoon fallback keeps Thursday’s last available block in reserve.
2. **Security patch.** The Friday deadline looks roomy until the review, half-day of QA and change window are placed in sequence. A Monday review gives QA a chance to test Tuesday and Engineering a chance to deploy Wednesday. Leaving the first review until midweek would make Thursday’s QA absence much more costly.
3. **Support escalations.** The renewal case has both an immediate customer consequence and the earliest response deadline, so it gets Monday’s first uninterrupted 45 minutes. Handling the other two Tuesday protects their Wednesday SLAs even if rehearsal or rollout needs all of Wednesday morning.

The CEO’s early-week request still gets three focused hours Monday. Moving the demo to the customer’s Thursday option would preserve Sam’s rehearsal slot and respect Wednesday’s hard stop.
