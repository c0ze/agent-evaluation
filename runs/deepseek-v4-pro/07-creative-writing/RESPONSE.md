The story moves from quiet dread to a release that is not rescue. Mara begins armored by procedure, treating the future message as a threat to verify; by the end she recognizes it as her own unsent fear, and the arc lands on acceptance rather than escape. The salt motif tracks this—it starts as an external nuisance and becomes the image of grief itself, patient and inevitable. The emotional turn is from control toward letting go, with the final image of the sea as a voice she will never fully hear.
