Issue 14 — Low Water — Autumn 2026

The River Keeps
Its Receipts

Imani Vale Field essay · 24 pages

From the editor

This issue follows the water down. Four dispatches from places that only become visible once something has left them — and a survey of what the riverbed keeps, item by item, in light.

Along a drought-exposed riverbed, shop ledgers, bottle caps, and vanished street plans surface together. A field essay about civic memory and what infrastructure tries to forget.

“What infrastructure tries to forget, the water gives back.”
