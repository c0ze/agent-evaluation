# Voice choices

**Precise.** Every factual sentence traces to `BRAND.md`: what the app does, where
processing happens, platforms, trial, price. Where the brief is silent (lab
pricing, Japanese, refunds), the copy says so instead of guessing.

**Warm.** Replies start by taking the commenter's point seriously ("Fair",
"Reasonable suspicion") and speak as "we". The outage reply apologises for the
specific thing that went wrong, asks only for non-sensitive details, and moves
account matters to DMs.

**Mildly nerdy.** One joke, the `final_v3_REAL.pdf` filename, on Instagram where
it fits. LinkedIn stays practical; Bluesky stays terse, matching its commenters.

**Never grandiose.** No superlatives, user counts, speed claims, or questions
fishing for comments. Linux is "investigating, not promising" everywhere it
appears.

`SOCIAL.md` ends with four items to confirm before posting, mainly that the
training reply matches the privacy policy.
