The arc is meant to run from bureaucratic numbness to a quiet, unheroic grief: Mira's competence is her armor, and the reader should feel the small horror of watching her apply it to her own mother's voice. The turn is built as a delayed click rather than a twist — the message stops being a threat and becomes an apology, releasing the dread the reader has been holding into tenderness. The last movement should land as relief and stubbornness at once: she keeps the tapes and lays down salt, a useless act of care that will have to be repeated.
