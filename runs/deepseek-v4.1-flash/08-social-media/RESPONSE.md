# Voice notes

The brief was precise, warm, mildly nerdy, never grandiose, so the copy leads with mechanics rather than excitement: what the app does, what it costs, where it runs. Sentences are short and concrete. No "revolutionary", no exclamation marks.

LinkedIn gets the longest treatment because that audience reads before clicking; the chatbot question is answered by separating a chat interface from a tool that points at sources. Bluesky replies stay clipped and plain-spoken to match the room, including accepting the Linux complaint without inventing a date. Instagram is warmer and more visual but still factual, with four specific hashtags instead of a wall of them.

Every reply answers the question asked. Where we lack information — Japanese support, lab licensing — we say so. The outage reply apologises once, gives a concrete next step, and moves account details to DM.
