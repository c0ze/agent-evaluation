# Moss — Design & Engineering Notes

## 1. Personality & Visual Direction

Moss is designed to feel like a **sunlit ceramic potting bench and beloved paper field journal** rather than a clinical productivity checklist or a glossy dashboard.

- **Tone of voice:** Warm, affectionate, optimistic, and gently odd. Overdue care is unmistakable without guilt-tripping — Frida’s Boston fern sits at the top of the queue in a terracotta-rimmed vessel (`2 days overdue`), accompanied by a quiet botanical observation (*“Loves shower steam; gets gently dramatic when her soil dries out”*) rather than harsh red error alerts.
- **Tactile materials & palette:** Unbleached linen paper (`#F3EDE0`) with a subtle CSS dot-weave grain, warm bone parchment surfaces (`#FCF9F2`), raw clay/terracotta (`#C24E2A` / `#933417`) for overdue urgency, deep botanical moss (`#2B5C43`) for calm completion, and toasted ochre (`#C98B20`) for sunlight and daily rounds. All typography uses local editorial serif, humanist sans, and monospace system stacks with `tabular-nums` for dates and counts — zero external fonts or remote assets.
- **Expressive shape language (no wall of identical cards):**
  - **Overdue hero vessel** (Frida) uses a wide terracotta-blush silhouette with a diagonal clay pot-lip rim, deeper clay offset shadow, and larger botanical portrait frame so overdue care commands immediate visual priority.
  - **Today’s gentle rounds** (Orbit and Dot) use subtly varied asymmetric pebble contours per plant (`data-plant="pothos"` vs. `data-plant="cactus"`) and pair into a 2-column sub-grid on wide desktop viewports.
  - **Coming up, snoozed & tended slips** sit in recessed linen pockets with dashed borders so resting plants remain reachable without competing with today’s work.
- **Small moments of delight (inline SVG botanical art):** Each plant has a custom mid-century/risograph-inspired inline SVG portrait that reacts directly to its state: Frida’s fronds tilt slightly lower when overdue and perk upright with dew sparkles when watered; Orbit’s trailing variegated pothos vine flips smoothly to the opposite side when rotated; Dot’s desert sun-arch brightens when soil is checked; Drama’s striped *Calathea orbifolia* leaves gain dotted mist arcs when misted.
- **Desktop composition:** Below `860px`, Moss flows as a thumb-friendly mobile-first stack with inline expandable specimen panels. At `860px+`, the layout opens into an asymmetric two-column potting-bench spread: the prioritized care queue and resting shelves on the left, paired with a sticky **Specimen Dossier** and the **Weekly Rhythm** pebble-stack widget on the right.

## 2. Interaction Model

- **Urgency-first care queue (`PLANTS.json`):** Loads `./PLANTS.json` locally with a seamless offline/`file://` fallback to the identical dataset. Plants are sorted by urgency descending: overdue (`Frida`), then today’s rounds (`Orbit`, `Dot`), with tomorrow’s plant (`Drama`) resting calmly below until needed (or actionable early via `Misted early`).
- **Task-true completion, watering & snooze:**
  - Each plant’s primary action matches its scheduled care (`Watered` for Frida, `Rotated` for Orbit, `Soil checked` for Dot, `Misted` for Drama), while non-water tasks also expose a `+ Log watered` chip inside the plant’s detail panel so watering can always be logged.
  - `Snooze` moves an active plant out of today’s queue into `Coming up & snoozed` for tomorrow and clears its urgency badge.
  - Every care action appends a freshly highlighted `"Today · …"` entry to that plant’s observation history and increments today’s column in the **Weekly Rhythm** chart live.
- **Brief, forgiving undo path:** Every state-changing action (care completion, snooze, extra watering, custom observation note, restore, and day reset) saves a full state snapshot and opens a floating dark-stoneware toast with a high-contrast `Undo` button and a `7.5s` draining progress bar. The countdown automatically pauses whenever the pointer hovers over or keyboard focus enters the toast, and `Ctrl+Z` / `Cmd+Z` reverts the latest action directly.
- **Specimen details & observation history:** Shows each plant’s room location, light intensity aperture (`1–3` pips), last watered date parsed from history, care cadence note, chronological ledger, and an inline `Add note` form to record new observations (e.g., new fronds or leaf edges).
- **Compact weekly rhythm visualization:** Renders the seven-day array (`[1, 3, 2, 4, 2, 1, 3]`) as stacked ceramic pebbles (`Mon`–`Today`) topped with a moss sprout on active/peak days. Selecting or focusing any day displays its exact count in the footer caption, and today’s stack adds a contrasting terracotta pebble as you complete care.
- **Useful all-done state:** Once all of today’s plants are tended or snoozed, the active queue transforms into a warm arched windowsill vignette summarizing how many plants were tended vs. snoozed, surfacing tomorrow’s next task (`Mist Drama`), and offering direct actions to peek at Drama’s dossier or replay today’s queue.

## 3. Accessibility Choices

- **Semantic structure & landmarks:** Uses `<header>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<aside>`, `<article>`, `<form>`, `<label>`, native `<button type="button">` controls, and a focus-revealed `"Skip to today’s care queue"` link.
- **Responsive ARIA parity:** Mobile uses inline expandable panels wired with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` while `desktop-dossier` is hidden both visually and via the `hidden` attribute; desktop hides mobile inline controls and uses `aria-current="true"` on the active plant feeding the sticky Specimen Dossier so assistive technologies never encounter duplicate controls.
- **Keyboard operation & focus management:** All actions, plant selectors, weekly rhythm day columns, and observation forms are operable via keyboard (`Tab`, `Enter`/`Space`, `Escape` to dismiss the toast or close an expanded mobile panel, and `Ctrl`/`⌘`+`Z` for Undo when not typing in an input). When a plant leaves the active queue after `Watered` or `Snooze`, focus advances intentionally to the next active plant’s primary action — or to the All-Done sanctuary when the final task completes — rather than dropping to `<body>`.
- **Visible focus & contrast:** Every interactive control uses a double-ring `:focus-visible` indicator (`3px` deep forest ink outline + `5px` warm parchment halo) that remains clearly legible against cream, terracotta, moss, and dark toast surfaces. Color pairings exceed WCAG AA contrast (primary ink on parchment ~14.5:1; deep moss and deep terracotta buttons with off-white text >5.5:1), and all touch targets meet a `44px` minimum height.
- **Live announcements & reduced motion:** Polite `aria-live="polite"` regions announce care completions, snoozes, saved notes, and undo reversals without interrupting speech. A `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block disables spring keyframes, hover rotations, button press translations, and draining bar animations while keeping all visual state changes crisp and immediate.
