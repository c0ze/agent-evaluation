The story moves Ines from hearing her future voice as a verdict, permission to keep staying and keep guarding other people's words, to hearing it as a loneliness she can still interrupt. The message's reassurance ("you did right, staying") is recast by the stairwell water and the untrimmed silence, so consolation turns into a request. I left the question of how the message exists unresolved on purpose, and let the emotional payoff be a small physical act: answering the phone and leaving the breathing in.
