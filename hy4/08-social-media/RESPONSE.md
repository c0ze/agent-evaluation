# Voice choices

I kept the brand voice — precise, warm, mildly nerdy, never grandiose — by leading with the checkable mechanic (source-linked summaries), not superlatives. Claims stay within BRAND.md: no invented counts, performance numbers, certifications, or dates. Linux is "investigated, not promised" throughout.

Posts differ by platform: LinkedIn explains the reasoning, Bluesky compresses to facts plus the caveat, Instagram is tactile with four hashtags and no engagement bait.

Replies answer the actual question with specifics. Where the honest answer is "no formal program," "not validated for Japanese," or "no Linux yet," I say so rather than soften it, offering a trial or a report instead of a promise. The chatbot and training comments get direct corrections. The indexing reply acknowledges the stall, skips canned gratitude, asks the user not to post private details, and offers a next step — reopen, then "Report a problem" — that sends diagnostics without exposing account details.
