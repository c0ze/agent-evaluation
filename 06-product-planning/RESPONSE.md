# Three riskiest assumptions

1. **Formal acceptance can wait for tablet erasure.** The plan assumes Operations can leave an inspection pending when a tablet is offline or lost, and Compliance accepts the fleet's erasure evidence. An immediate acceptance requirement would make the 24-hour deadline impossible to guarantee with cleanup that depends on reconnecting. Confirm the workflow and verify erasure on the managed fleet before implementation.
2. **Inspectors can prepare for offline work and sync within field conditions.** The plan assumes assignments are known before a dead zone, tablets can hold a complete inspection with 40 photos and a signature, and later connection windows are sufficient for resumable sync. Validate predownload, storage and reconnection on representative shifts and tablets to set the pilot's limits and recovery targets.
3. **Concurrent edits can be resolved with field-level review.** The plan assumes most inspector and supervisor changes are independent, and overlapping or signature-invalidating edits can go back to a named person. Walk through real forms and signed evidence with both roles. Frequent conflicts or impractical re-signing would require changing when supervisors can edit or what they can edit during the pilot.
