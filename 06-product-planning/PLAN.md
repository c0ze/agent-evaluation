# Offline building inspections

An inspector can download an assigned inspection before entering a basement, complete the form with up to 40 photos and a signature, and leave the app safely while the tablet waits for a connection. The server records what the inspector and supervisor changed. It records acceptance only after confirming that every tablet copy has been erased.

## User flow and pilot scope

1. On a connection, the inspector signs in and downloads assigned inspections. Show **Ready offline** per inspection after its form template, reference data, current record and any required media are durably stored and the tablet passes its storage check. Show incomplete downloads and retry them. Assignments made after disconnection become available on the next connection.
2. Offline, the inspector opens a ready inspection, fills the form, takes or removes photos, and captures the signature. Save edits and photo references locally as the inspector works. Show **Saved on tablet** only after the local commit. If storage or capture fails, retain previous work and make the failed item visible. Reopening the app restores the draft and its sync status.
3. The inspector can submit offline. Show **Waiting to send** after recording the intent for a specific draft revision. The inspector can withdraw an unsent submission to resume editing. If a submission request is already in flight, resolve its outcome before allowing withdrawal. On a connection, sync the form and media and send the submission. Show photo progress and actionable states for reauthentication, rejected input or conflicting edits. Keep the draft and submission intent across app restarts. Show **Submitted for review** once the server confirms a complete submission. The web supervisor reviews the submitted revision and can return it for changes.
4. When review is complete, the supervisor requests acceptance. Show **Awaiting tablet cleanup** in the web app until the server receives all erasure receipts. The tablet shows a status-only confirmation after cleanup and can display the server's acceptance confirmation on a later connection.

The pilot supports assigned inspections, one editing tablet per inspection, and concurrent supervisor edits on the web. Reserve the tablet edit slot before download and keep it across connection loss. Transfer it through an explicit handoff after pending work is synced or accounted for. Later scope includes offline assignment or inspection creation, editing from several tablets, live collaboration, and offline acceptance.

## Decisions before implementation

These recommendations are planning assumptions. Confirm them with the people who own the workflow and the systems before committing to implementation.

| Decision | Recommendation and evidence to confirm it |
| --- | --- |
| Acceptance and the 24-hour deadline | Product, Operations and Compliance should confirm that formal acceptance can wait for all tablet erasure receipts and that erasure before acceptance satisfies the deadline. Confirm what counts as local customer data and acceptable erasure evidence, including whether destruction of a device-bound key qualifies. Walk through a tablet that stays offline beyond 24 hours, a lost tablet and a reassignment. If acceptance must proceed while a copy may remain, make a verified alternative a release prerequisite. |
| Offline readiness and device use | Start with predownloaded assignments and one named inspector per managed tablet. Have field teams confirm they can connect before a shift and eventually reconnect. Measure the longest offline sessions, largest supported forms and typical photo sizes on representative tablets. Use those measurements to set a download storage reserve, offline access window and sync recovery targets. |
| Concurrent edits | Merge disjoint changes to independent fields. Require a named reviewer to resolve overlapping edits and changes to dependent field groups. Product should approve the field grouping, who resolves each overlap, and when work returns to the inspector. Exercise those rules on real inspection forms. |
| Signature and audit | Compliance and Product should specify the signed content, signer identification and consent evidence, audit retention, and access roles. Bind the signature to the exact approved form and evidence snapshot. Require a fresh signature when signed content changes. Confirm whether offline capture time is acceptable and how it should be represented. |
| Photo and form compatibility | Approve photo quality, allowed formats, metadata retention and a maximum byte size that preserves evidence. Select the initial form types and agree how long the server will honor their downloaded schema versions. Verify that a full 40-photo inspection fits and can finish syncing across representative connection windows. |
| Managed-device capabilities and access | Verify device-bound encryption, offline unlock behavior and remotely verifiable erase on the supported tablet fleet. Agree on a bounded offline session and an authorized recovery path for revoked access or stranded drafts. Require an operator-reviewed exception path for lost devices before recruiting the pilot. |

## Local storage

Use SQLite as durable working storage. Keep customer content in an encrypted per-inspection store with a device-bound key. Keep a small control store for the minimum opaque identifiers and sync state needed to finish cleanup and retry acknowledgments. Have Compliance review the fields retained after erasure.

| Data | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Inspection and draft | Inspection ID, account and tablet registration, download generation, base server version and snapshot, pinned form schema, draft values, dirty fields, and review or submission state. |
| Outbox | Persistent mutation IDs, expected versions, intents and immutable payloads for requests that may have reached the server. Include retry state and the most recent acknowledged operation. |
| Attachments and signature | Stable client IDs, encrypted file references, size and digest, upload session and confirmed progress, capture metadata, and the signature's bound snapshot and consent evidence. |
| Cleanup | Generation fence and opaque cleanup challenge or receipt retry state. Persist the fence before erasing the inspection store so a restart or delayed response cannot restore it. |

Commit each form edit and its pending sync state together. Write media to a durable encrypted file before committing its SQLite reference. On restart, reconcile interrupted writes and reclaim unreferenced files. Persist server acknowledgments and the new base together while retaining any newer local edits. Protect drafts from normal cache eviction and app upgrade. An unsynced draft has one durable copy until it reaches the server, so Operations must treat a lost or physically failed tablet as a possible loss of unsynced work.

## Sync and conflict handling

- **Download and ownership.** Register a per-device copy and download generation on the server before returning the snapshot or accessing its media. Retries reuse the registration. Include the integer record version, form schema version and download manifest. Use the same registration rules for every tablet path that can read pilot inspection data. Track copy ownership separately from permission to edit.
- **Mutations.** Have the tablet send REST patches against the last acknowledged version using `If-Match` and a persistent operation ID scoped to the inspection and tablet. Send one record mutation at a time per inspection. The server authenticates the caller, checks the operation ID before evaluating the version on an authorized replay, and returns the original result for a matching request. Reject reuse of the ID with a different payload. Commit the revision, new integer version, audit event and operation result in one transaction. Keep replay records for the supported retry window. Beyond it, reconcile unknown outcomes with the server before offering another send. Apply conditional version checks and audit logging to supervisor writes too.
- **Retries.** Resume on app launch, foregrounding and reconnect, with OS background work when available. Use bounded exponential backoff with jitter for transport failures and retryable server responses. Pause on expired credentials and request sign-in. Keep field-validation errors visible in the form. An interrupted response is an unknown outcome, so retry the same operation ID and payload. Refresh after an acknowledged replay to detect subsequent supervisor edits.
- **Conflicts.** On version mismatch fetch the latest snapshot and compare it with the saved base and local draft. Accept matching changes and merge disjoint edits to independent fields, such as a note and an unrelated checkbox. Treat overlapping values, delete-versus-edit, and dependent field groups as conflicts. Preserve both values and their provenance. The inspector resolves allowed fields and the supervisor resolves supervisor-controlled values. Record the choice and actor and send the resolution against the latest version. Another intervening edit repeats the check. A change to signed content invalidates the signature for submission or acceptance and sends the inspection back for re-signing. Keep prior signatures in the restricted server audit history with their original revision.
- **Submission.** Use a versioned, idempotent submission operation. The server checks the resolved form against its pinned schema, all referenced media, the photo limit, and signature binding. It then records the exact reviewable revision. A later web change creates a distinct audited revision and triggers any required revalidation or re-signing. Keep an unsupported schema draft on the tablet and route it to an audited recovery flow.

## Photos and signature

Capture photos directly into app-controlled storage. Apply the approved evidence settings and enforce the 40-photo limit on the tablet and server. Track each photo independently so successful uploads survive a connection drop elsewhere in the batch. Start or resume an upload by stable attachment ID, query acknowledged progress and retry only missing parts. Verify the final length and digest on the server before allowing the record to reference the photo. Use authenticated, inspection-scoped uploads. Expire abandoned uploads, with a restart path from the local copy if staging expires. Retain committed evidence under the approved audit policy.

Store the signature in the encrypted inspection store and upload it under the same integrity and access controls. Bind it to a canonical snapshot of approved fields and evidence digests, alongside signer, consent and capture information. If an inspector edits that snapshot after signing, clear its signed status locally and prompt for a fresh signature. Record the device-reported capture time and the server receipt time as distinct values. Preserve the signed snapshot through reconciliation so the server can verify the binding before submission and acceptance.

## Security, audit and erasure

Use transport encryption, server-side role and assignment checks, and named inspector and supervisor accounts. Require managed device unlock and a bounded offline session for cached access. Expiry locks access to the draft while retaining it for authorized recovery. Recheck account, assignment and device status on reconnect before allowing uploads or downloads. A disconnected tablet can enforce its local expiration, and receives new revocation instructions when it reconnects.

Keep customer content in app-private encrypted storage, including SQLite journals, media working files and thumbnails. Exclude it from OS backups and shared photo storage. Restrict inspector tablets to data paths covered by device registration and disable persistent caching on the web review surface. Keep form content, images, signatures and customer identifiers out of operational telemetry. The restricted server audit trail records actor, source, operation ID, previous and resulting revisions, changes or revision references, conflict choices, signature events, submission, cleanup evidence and acceptance. Use server timestamps for authoritative event ordering and apply the approved retention policy.

1. On an acceptance request, the server checks that review is complete and freezes the review revision. It stops issuing tablet reads and writes for that generation and records a cleanup challenge for each registered copy. The tablet checks for pending local work before cleanup. Unexpected unsent work keeps acceptance pending. The supervisor cancels that attempt and routes the work through review or authorized recovery before trying again.
2. The tablet stops writers, fences the retired generation, and erases the inspection's database, media, signature, temporary files, keys and any legacy copies covered by the registration. Erasure follows the method approved for the managed fleet. Resume interrupted cleanup after restart. Retry the authenticated receipt with the opaque challenge if delivery fails. Authorize cleanup separately from inspection access so a reassigned tablet can still erase and acknowledge its copy. A verified managed-device erase can provide evidence for a lost tablet if Compliance approves that mechanism. An unreachable device without evidence keeps acceptance pending.
3. In the acceptance transaction, verify the frozen revision and all receipts, then record the authoritative acceptance time and audit event. Serve only status to tablets from that point. Serialize copy registration, acceptance and cancellation so a late download or older cleanup receipt cannot satisfy a newer attempt. Reopening invalidates the acceptance attempt and uses a fresh download generation for further tablet work. Retain registrations for any older copies still awaiting verified erasure, and scope cleanup to the targeted copy.

## Migration and backfill

Deploy the additive server schema, web write guards and audit handling before enabling tablets. Seed each included inspection's current server revision as a migration baseline, keeping any existing history with its provenance. Use the actual migration time and existing record versions. Use the same transaction guard for the backfill and web writes so a concurrent edit establishes the baseline before recording its new revision. Make the backfill restartable and verify revision and audit counts before enrolling a cohort.

Upgrade enrolled tablets while connected. Check that any work from the cache-based app is committed or recovered before retiring its cache. Populate the durable store from a verified server snapshot and validate the schema and media manifest. Reconcile the legacy device inventory with Operations. Start with assignments whose earlier tablet copies can be ruled out or have documented erasure. Require the new registration and acceptance rules for all tablet clients that can read enrolled inspections. Block enrollment if a legacy client or cache remains unaccounted for.

## Observability, rollout and rollback

Track offline-readiness failures, local save errors, queued work age, retry outcomes, upload completion, storage pressure, version conflicts, signature invalidations and time spent awaiting cleanup. Track how often uploads resume, plus delay from reconnect to server acknowledgment. Report device-only events when connectivity returns. Reconcile acceptance events with device registrations and purge receipts. Alert on an accepted inspection with missing erasure evidence, a customer data exposure or suspected loss of a saved draft. Assign an on-call owner and a field support contact before the pilot. Set quantitative performance and queue-age thresholds using field measurements before enabling the pilot.

Release the server changes with enrollment disabled, then run a managed-device field simulation. Enable a small pilot cohort of inspectors, supervisors and representative tablets and form types. Review sync and cleanup metrics alongside field feedback. Expand in cohorts once every acceptance has complete erasure evidence, field performance meets the agreed thresholds, and the pilot has no unresolved saved-work loss, evidence-integrity or security defects. Pause enrollment if any of these gates fail.

Use separate controls for new downloads and draining existing work. On rollback, stop new enrollment and downloads first. Keep compatible sync, audit and cleanup paths available for enrolled tablets to upload, resolve and purge. If a server path is unsafe, pause that operation and preserve encrypted drafts until a fixed compatible version can reconcile them. Keep pending acceptances gated and record operator actions in the audit trail. Verify outbox, upload and cleanup state before retiring a client or server version. Practice this sequence with a tablet offline and an inspection awaiting cleanup.

## Verification cases

- **Local durability and upgrade.** Exercise interrupted prefetch, force quit during SQLite commit or photo capture, OS reboot, low storage, and upgrade with a queued submission. Compare restored drafts and attachment checksums to the saved originals. Confirm that the app identifies work that has yet to be saved.
- **Network and API behavior.** Interrupt uploads between parts and drop responses after a committed mutation, submission or cleanup receipt. Replay operations after a supervisor edit, expire upload sessions and credentials, and run through the supported retry window. Verify server versions, unique audit events and no duplicate attachments.
- **Forms, signatures and conflicts.** Test independent edits, collisions, dependent fields, photo removal, outdated schemas and a web edit that changes signed content. Assert that neither side's evidence is discarded and that acceptance uses the reviewed revision with a valid signature.
- **Device access and erasure.** Test expired access, reassignment, unauthorized roles, disabled devices, multiple registered copies, restart during purge and a late response arriving after cleanup. Inspect restored backups and device storage for customer data. Keep a tablet offline beyond 24 hours after the acceptance request and verify that the acceptance timestamp is still unset. Confirm that a verified erase receipt allows the normal acceptance transaction.
- **Field and rollback.** Run full inspections on representative managed tablets and poor connections. Measure storage, upload progress and recovery time. Disable enrollment mid-sync and prove that existing drafts, audit records and cleanup work can be drained through the supported versions.
