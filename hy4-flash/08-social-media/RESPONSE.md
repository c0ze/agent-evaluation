# Voice choices

Posts and replies stay precise, warm, and mildly nerdy, never grandiose, as BRAND.md asks.

Launch posts lead with a concrete mechanism — a summary sentence you can click through to its source — not superlatives. I invented no customer counts, performance numbers, certifications, or roadmap dates. Linux is "investigating, not promising," and "14-day trial, then $12/month" states price without a hard sell.

Replies answer the actual question and drop canned "thanks for your feedback" prose. Where I couldn't help — no lab licensing, no Linux date, unvalidated Japanese summaries — I said so plainly rather than implying a feature exists. On privacy I was direct: no uploading, no training on user files.

For the outage reply, I acknowledged the problem, apologized, offered a public troubleshooting step (batch the folder to isolate a stall), and moved account specifics to direct messages so no private details appear publicly.
